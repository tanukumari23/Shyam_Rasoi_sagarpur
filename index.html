Our Story

Ghar Jaisa Pyaar, Dhaba Jaisa Swaad

Born in the bustling lanes of Sagarpur, Shyam Rasoi brings the rustic, smoky flavours of a traditional Punjabi dhaba right to your plate — at prices everyone can enjoy.

Every dish is cooked fresh with hand-picked spices in a hygienic kitchen by chefs who treat cooking as an art. Whether you walk in with family, drive through after work, or order online, we promise warmth, taste, and a true taste of India in every bite.
